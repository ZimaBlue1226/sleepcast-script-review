# General English SleepCast review standards

Judge whether the English sounds originally written for North American adults, then whether it is safe, clear, immersive and consistent with its genre. Grammatical correctness alone is insufficient. Preserve the defining creative experience; low stimulation does not require complete stillness.

## Natural North American expression and listening

- Grammar, spelling, tense, viewpoint and referents are coherent.
- Collocations, sentence construction and transitions sound natural rather than translated, explanatory or templated.
- Meaning is readily understood without seeing the text.
- Imagery does not create unintended threatening, childish or otherwise incongruous associations in context.
- Narration fits adult SleepCast listening. Natural prose fragments are not automatically grammar errors.

## Adult sleep suitability

- Avoid frightening, coercive, trapped or falling experiences, excessive excitement and sustained arousing developments.
- Use gentle, permissive narration; do not create obligations to stay alert or solve a problem.
- Allow interaction, narrative events and environmental change when their intensity and presentation remain suitable.
- Judge perceived safety from the complete passage, not a list of banned words.

## Narrative and perceptual logic

- Motivations, action order and causal connections are clear where applicable.
- Transitions and spatial relationships are coherent.
- Establish a new environment before demanding attention to isolated detail.
- Ground information in what the listener can perceive; avoid unnecessary explanations of backstage creative rules.

## Genre and content consistency

- Setting, narrative treatment and content logic agree throughout.
- Core elements emerge through scenes, characters, events or narration, as appropriate to the genre.
- Do not impose an unrelated genre's requirements.
- Characters, environment and interaction support the theme where applicable.
- Repairs preserve the script's distinctive experience.

## Pacing and listening continuity

- Balance development, environmental dwelling and gradual sleep descent.
- Identify information overload, prolonged stagnation and imagery that repeats without purpose.
- Evaluate repeated syntax, state verbs and slow-motion adverbs in context.
- Sentence length, punctuation and rhythm support continuous listening; no numerical sentence or line quota is imposed here.

Inspect language and comprehension first, then sleep suitability, logic, genre and pacing. This inspection order does not determine P0/P1/P2. Before deleting a core interaction, consider reducing intensity, changing the perceptual viewpoint or repairing its language.
